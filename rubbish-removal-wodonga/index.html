Your options for rubbish removal in Wodonga

In short: For a big clean-up, a skip bin is usually the simplest way to remove household or garden waste. Wodonga Council doesn't offer a hard waste kerbside collection, so the other option is the Resource Recovery Centre and your free tipping vouchers.

Most Wodonga households have two ways to get rid of more rubbish than fits in their bins:

  1. A skip bin, delivered to your driveway and taken away when you're done.
  2. Taking it to the tip yourself, at Wodonga Council's Resource Recovery Centre on Kane Road, using your two free tipping vouchers.

Which one makes sense depends on how much you have, what it is, and whether you have a trailer and the time to do the runs.

Option 1: a skip bin

In short: A skip suits jobs with a lot of mixed rubbish, heavy waste or many trailer loads of garden waste, because you load it at your own pace and it's taken away in one go.

A skip sits on your driveway (or on the street with council approval) for an agreed hire period. You can fill it over a few days, and you don't need a trailer, a ute or anyone to lift heavy items. That matters in Wodonga, because council doesn't offer a hard waste kerbside collection. Skips can take furniture, bagged household rubbish, garden waste, soil, bricks and renovation waste, as long as you choose the right bin and tell the operator. They can't take asbestos, gas bottles, batteries, tyres or chemicals in a normal load. See what can go in a skip and the size guide.

For a deceased estate, a house move or a big shed or garage job, see our clean-outs guide.

Option 2: the Wodonga Resource Recovery Centre

In short: If you have a trailer and only a load or two, you can take rubbish to council's Resource Recovery Centre yourself. Sorting your load reduces what goes to landfill.

Wodonga Council's Wodonga Resource Recovery Centre is at 29 Kane Road. Council lists it as open 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday and 10am to 4pm on Saturday and Sunday. It's closed on Good Friday, Easter Sunday, Anzac Day, Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. The centre is designed to maximise re-use and recycling, council runs a Sort n Save approach, and there's a re-use shop on site. Council says the centre also accepts many bulkier items, such as polystyrene and large amounts of cardboard, that fill your bins quickly.

Council provides two free tipping vouchers each year, attached to rates notices, for occupied residential properties. In Victoria, e-waste can't go in any bin or landfill, so take old electronics to an e-waste drop-off point such as the Resource Recovery Centre.

Is there a hard rubbish collection in Wodonga?

In short: No. Wodonga Council says a hard waste kerbside collection service isn't offered.

Wodonga Council's bins and collection page says a hard waste kerbside collection is not offered in Wodonga. Instead, it points residents to the Resource Recovery Centre and the two free tipping vouchers on your rates notice. Good-condition furniture and household goods can go to the centre's re-use shop.

What about my kerbside bins?

In short: Your bins are for everyday rubbish, recycling and food and garden waste, not clean-ups.

The standard Wodonga service is a 240 L green-lid organics bin collected weekly, and a 140 L red-lid rubbish bin and a 240 L yellow-lid recycling bin collected on alternate fortnights. Council says bins that are too heavy may not be collected. Soil, treated timber and general household waste can't go in the organics bin.

So which is easier?

In short: Pick a skip for big, mixed or heavy jobs. Use the Resource Recovery Centre and your vouchers for a load or two.

  • A skip bin usually makes sense for a full clean-out, a renovation, a big garden or shed job, heavy waste, or when you don't have a trailer or the time for several tip runs.
  • The Resource Recovery Centre suits one or two trailer loads, especially while you still have your tipping vouchers.
  • The re-use shop suits furniture and household goods that are still in good condition.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to get rid of a lot of rubbish in Wodonga?

It depends on how much you have and what it is. For one or two loads, a trailer run to the Resource Recovery Centre on Kane Road, using your free tipping vouchers, may be enough. For a big clean-out, heavy waste or many loads, a skip bin is often the easier option, because you load it at your own pace and it's taken away in one trip.

Where is the Wodonga tip and when is it open?

Wodonga Council's Resource Recovery Centre is at 29 Kane Road. Council lists it as open 9am to 5pm on weekdays and 10am to 4pm on weekends. It's closed on Good Friday, Easter Sunday, Anzac Day, Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

Does Wodonga Council have a hard rubbish collection?

No. Council says a hard waste kerbside collection service isn't offered in Wodonga. Bulky items can go to the Resource Recovery Centre.

Do I get free tip vouchers in Wodonga?

Yes. Council provides two free tipping vouchers each year, attached to rates notices, for occupied residential properties.

Can I put old electronics in a skip or my bin in Wodonga?

No. Since 1 July 2019, e-waste can't go in any bin or landfill in Victoria. Take it to an e-waste drop-off point such as the Resource Recovery Centre.

Can a skip bin take garden waste and building rubbish?

Usually, yes, which is one reason people choose a skip. Heavy waste such as soil, concrete and bricks normally needs a smaller bin with a weight limit, and asbestos can never go in a general skip. Tell the operator what you have when you request a quote.

Do I need a permit for a skip bin on the street in Wodonga?

A skip wholly on your own property generally doesn't need council approval. A skip on the road, footpath or verge usually needs approval from Wodonga Council. See our council skip permits guide for details.
