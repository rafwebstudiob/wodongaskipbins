Skip bins in Killara

Killara is about 8 km from central Wodonga by road. Here's what to know before you hire a skip, and how to get a free quote.

Last checked 24 October 2026

Skip hire in Killara

Killara is a newer suburb on Wodonga's eastern side, with plenty of recent homes. Landscaping, builders' waste and garage clean-outs are common.

Nearest tip

Wodonga Council's Resource Recovery Centre is at 29 Kane Road. Council lists it as open Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm and weekends 10am to 4pm, closed Good Friday, Easter Sunday, Anzac Day, Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. Many recyclables are free to drop off, and general waste, green waste, timber, mattresses and tyres are accepted for a fee. It doesn't accept asbestos, cement sheeting or household chemicals. Call the centre on (02) 6022 9358 if you're unsure, and check the Wodonga Council website for fees.

Council rules

A skip wholly on your own land generally doesn't need approval. On the road or nature strip, Wodonga Council requires a skip bin permit, listed at $54.70 for 2025–26. See the permit guide.
