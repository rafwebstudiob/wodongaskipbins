What can and can't go in a skip in Wodonga

What goes in the bin changes the price and whether the operator can take it. Here's what's usually accepted, what isn't, and where Wodonga households can take problem waste instead.

Last checked 24 October 2026

Usually accepted

  • General household waste: furniture, toys, clothes, boxes and general junk.
  • Green waste: gum branches, prunings, lawn clippings and hedge trimmings. A green-only bin is often cheaper than mixed waste, so keep soil and rubbish out of it.
  • Renovation waste: timber, plasterboard, old cabinets, carpet, tiles and fittings (but not fibro that may contain asbestos).
  • Heavy or clean fill: soil, sand, concrete, bricks and pavers, usually in a smaller bin with a weight limit.
  • Scrap metal: old roofing iron, fencing wire, steel posts and outdoor furniture.

Not accepted, or extra charge

  • Asbestos and fibro: never in a general skip. See below.
  • Tyres: usually banned or charged per tyre.
  • Batteries: car and household batteries are a fire risk in a skip.
  • Gas bottles, fire extinguishers and aerosols: can explode when compacted.
  • Paint, oils, chemicals and liquids: including pool chemicals, farm chemicals, sump oil and paint.
  • Mattresses: often accepted for an extra fee.
  • E-waste, food waste and medical waste: check first. Many operators won't take TVs, computers or fluorescent tubes.

Every operator has its own list. The one who quotes you will confirm theirs, so mention anything unusual in the form.

Asbestos and fibro in Wodonga

Many older Wodonga homes and sheds have fibro sheeting or asbestos-cement roofing. You can't tell by looking, so if a sheet might be fibro, treat it as asbestos. In Victoria, the state's asbestos guidance (asbestos.vic.gov.au) says a homeowner may remove up to 10 m² of non-friable (bonded) asbestos, and that friable asbestos must be removed by a WorkSafe-licensed removalist. For anything bigger, or if you're unsure, use a licensed removalist. EPA Victoria requires asbestos waste to be wrapped, labelled and taken to a site authorised to accept it, never hidden in a general skip. In Wodonga, council's Resource Recovery Centre doesn't accept asbestos or cement sheeting, so use a licensed removalist or find an authorised facility with the national disposal-facility finder and call ahead.

Where to take problem waste

Wodonga Council's Resource Recovery Centre is at 29 Kane Road. Council lists it as open Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm and weekends 10am to 4pm, closed Good Friday, Easter Sunday, Anzac Day, Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. Many recyclables are free to drop off, and general waste, green waste, timber, mattresses and tyres are accepted for a fee. It doesn't accept asbestos, cement sheeting or household chemicals. Call the centre on (02) 6022 9358 if you're unsure, and check the Wodonga Council website for fees.

Tips for a cheaper, easier skip

  • Separate green waste and heavy waste from general rubbish where you can.
  • Break down furniture and flatten boxes so they take less space.
  • Don't fill above the rim. Operators can refuse to lift an overfilled bin.
  • Be upfront about mattresses, tyres or anything you're unsure about.
